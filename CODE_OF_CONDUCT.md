# MoniCheck Community Guidelines

MoniCheck welcomes practical, evidence-backed collaboration from operators,
developers, security researchers, and documentation contributors.

## Expected Conduct

- Discuss technical decisions and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and authorization boundaries.
- Do not post credentials, private endpoints, customer reports, resource names,
  or incident details in public threads.
- State whether evidence comes from a real authorized environment, a fixture,
  or an inference.
- Accept correction, disclose uncertainty, and keep product claims measurable.
- Give contributors appropriate credit and respect licensing obligations.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private data,
spam, and deceptive product or security claims are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict
participation, or report abuse to the hosting platform. Security issues must use
the private process in `SECURITY.md`.

For conduct concerns that cannot be discussed publicly, request a private
maintainer channel without including sensitive details in the public request.
